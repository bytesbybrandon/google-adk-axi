# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project aims to provide a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or personal circumstances.

Community members are expected to act with empathy, respect differing viewpoints, give and accept constructive feedback, take responsibility for their actions, and protect other people's private information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention without consent, personal attacks, doxxing, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone represents the project in public.

Report abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior to [brandon@bytesbybrandon.com](mailto:brandon@bytesbybrandon.com).

Project leaders will review reports promptly and fairly, protect the reporter's privacy, explain moderation decisions when appropriate, and may remove contributions or restrict participation in response to violations.

Responses may include a correction request, a warning, a temporary participation ban, or a permanent ban, based on the impact and pattern of the behavior.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
